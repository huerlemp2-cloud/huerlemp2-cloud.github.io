Astrology and Ritual Disclaimer

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-01

What the results mean

Election Magick applies a documented traditional method of electional astrology (described in Settings → Methodology). Its classifications ("Excellent", "Favourable", "Weak" …), risk levels and rankings describe how a moment scores within that method. They are not scientific findings, predictions or promises. The app does not claim, and cannot guarantee, that any ritual, intention or action will succeed or have any effect.

Not professional advice

Nothing in the app is medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal, financial, tax or other professional advice. Do not delay or replace professional help because of anything shown in the app. If you are in crisis or at risk, contact local emergency services.

Entities and sources

The entity library summarises how historical, religious and literary sources describe spirits, deities and other figures. Every statement shows its source level: Attested (found in the historical source), Reconstruction (a plausible reconstruction) or Modern (a modern association). Inclusion does not mean that an entity exists, that a practice is endorsed, or that a source is accurate. Respect the beliefs of others and of living traditions.

Your responsibility

You decide whether and how to practise any ritual. Take care with fire, candles, incense, oils, plants, foods and any other materials; follow the law and the safety instructions for anything you use; never put yourself or others at risk. Do not use the app to harm, threaten or deceive anyone.

Calculation limits

Positions come from a published astronomical library and are checked against an independent ephemeris, but every calculation has limits: house systems and planetary hours are undefined at some high latitudes (the app shows this and never estimates them), time-zone data comes from the device's database, and any method choices are documented, not universal. Check important dates yourself.

AI explanations

Explanations written by Apple Intelligence describe results the app already calculated and are checked automatically, but they may still contain errors of wording. The calculated values always take precedence.
