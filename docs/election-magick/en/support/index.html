Support

Contact: huerlem.peters1988@outlook.com

Subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Manage or cancel: Settings → Manage Subscription, or iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: Settings → Request a refund from Apple, or https://reportaproblem.apple.com

Your data

  • Everything is stored on your device. Settings → Delete local data removes it.
  • The Privacy Policy explains location, Apple Maps, AI explanations and Private Cloud Compute.

Calculations

  • Methodology and sources are in the app under Settings → Methodology and Settings → Sources.
  • At very high latitudes houses fall back to Whole Sign and planetary hours can be unavailable on some days; the app always shows this and never estimates them.
