Subscription Terms

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-01

These Subscription Terms form part of the Terms of Use of the Election Magick app. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store and are also subject to Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions.

1. Plans

The app offers two plans, each billed monthly or annually:

  • Standard Monthly and Standard Annual
  • Premium Monthly and Premium Annual

The astrology calculation engine is identical on both plans. Premium is not "more accurate"; it gives deeper analysis, longer search ranges, more ranked results and more consultations:

  • Consultations per usage period: Standard 10, Premium 50.
  • Longest search range: Standard 14 days, Premium 92 days.
  • Ranked elections displayed: Standard 3, Premium 7 (both plans always compare at least five complete elections).
  • Classification: Standard shows Favourable and lower labels; when an election qualifies above that range, Standard shows "High-quality election found" and Premium shows the full label (Excellent or Very Favourable). A label is never changed to a lower one.
  • Risk audit and Moon sequence: Standard shows the problems found and the next lunar aspect; Premium shows every rule with orbs and justifications and the full lunar sequence to the sign change.
  • Entity compatibility: basic on Standard, full with source classification on Premium.
  • Report export: basic on Standard, full on Premium.
  • Saved elections: up to 25 on Standard, unlimited on Premium.

2. Prices

Prices, currency and any taxes are shown by the App Store before you confirm a purchase and on your receipt. They are set per country in App Store Connect and are never fixed in the app.

3. Introductory offers

If an introductory offer (for example a free trial) is available to you, the App Store shows its length and the price that applies afterwards before you subscribe. Eligibility is decided by Apple.

4. Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period at the price then in force, unless you turn off automatic renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple account is charged within the 24 hours before renewal. If the price increases, Apple informs you and, where required, asks for your consent.

5. Cancelling and managing

Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, or from the app (Settings → Manage Subscription). Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period already paid.

6. Changing plan

You can move between Standard and Premium or between monthly and annual billing through the same subscription settings. Apple applies upgrades immediately (with a pro-rated refund of the old plan) and downgrades or cross-grades at the next renewal date.

7. Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com or from the app (Settings → Request a refund from Apple). We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves. You may also have mandatory statutory cancellation or consumer rights under the laws applicable to you; Apple's refund process does not remove those rights. If Apple grants a refund, access to the refunded subscription ends.

8. Consultations

One consultation is one new calculation that produces a result: analysing a moment, or searching for an election, including a new run with a materially different intent, entity, place, date range, house system or advanced options. The following never use a consultation: reopening a saved election or history item, changing language, viewing or exporting reports, reading the methodology or entity pages, restoring purchases, or a calculation that fails or is rejected before it runs.

Usage periods follow your subscription: monthly plans use the billing month; annual plans use monthly periods counted from your purchase date. Unused consultations do not carry over. The count is kept on your device (see the Privacy Policy) and is not reset by deleting local data.

9. Billing problems

If a renewal payment fails, Apple may retry it. During Apple's billing grace period your access continues; if the problem is not resolved, access ends until payment succeeds.

10. After a subscription ends

Your saved elections, places and reports stay on your device. New calculations, reopening saved results and the entity library require an active subscription again.

11. Family Sharing

The subscriptions are not shared through Family Sharing.
