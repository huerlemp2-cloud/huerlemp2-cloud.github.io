# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-01

This Privacy Policy explains how the Election Magick app ("the app") handles information. The app is provided by [[OWNER_LEGAL_NAME]], [[OWNER_POSTAL_ADDRESS]] ("we", "us"). Contact: [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]].

In short: the app works on your device. We do not run servers, we have no user accounts, and we do not receive your astrology data, your location or your ritual descriptions. Some features use Apple services, described below.

## 1. Calculations happen on your device

<!-- fact:P-CALC-1 -->
All astronomical and astrological calculations (planet positions, houses, planetary hours, risk checks, classifications and rankings) run on your device. No calculation input or result is sent to us. We do not operate any server for the app.

## 2. Location

<!-- fact:P-LOC-1 -->
Using your location is optional. The app asks for permission only after explaining why, only "while using the app", and only at approximate accuracy (about 1 km). You can always choose a city instead; every feature works without location access.

<!-- fact:P-LOC-2 -->
If you choose "Use current location", the app takes a fresh position each time you calculate. To find the place name and its time zone, your coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service (Apple Maps). The app does not store positions obtained this way unless you save the place.

<!-- fact:P-LOC-3 -->
When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to show suggestions. The city you choose (name, country, coordinates and time zone) is stored on your device so you can reuse it.

Apple's handling of Maps requests is described in Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 3. Information stored on your device

<!-- fact:P-DATA-1 -->
Saved elections, saved places, favourite entities, ritual profiles and your calculation history are stored only on your device. They can include the ritual description you typed, the entity you chose, the place's coordinates, name and time zone, and the dates calculated.

<!-- fact:P-DATA-2 -->
Your preferences (language, house system, lunar node, whether AI explanations are on) are stored on your device.

<!-- fact:P-DATA-3 -->
When you accept the Terms of Use and acknowledge this Privacy Policy, the app records on your device which versions you accepted, when, and the app version.

## 4. Subscriptions and purchases

<!-- fact:P-SUB-1 -->
Subscriptions are sold, billed, renewed, cancelled and refunded by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment details; we never see them. The app receives from Apple only verified transaction information it needs to unlock your plan: the product, purchase and expiry dates, transaction identifiers and renewal state.

<!-- fact:P-SUB-2 -->
Each plan includes a number of consultations per period. To count them, the app stores in your device's Keychain (on this device only) a record per subscription period made of one-way digests (SHA-256) of the consultations already used. The digests do not contain your ritual text, place or dates in readable form. This record is kept when you use "Delete local data", so that the allowance cannot be reset, and because of how iOS works it may remain in the Keychain after you delete the app.

## 5. AI explanations

<!-- fact:P-AI-1 -->
AI never performs astrology calculations and never changes a result. It only writes explanations of results that the app has already calculated.

<!-- fact:P-AI-2 -->
By default, explanations are written by Apple Intelligence on your device (Apple Foundation Models). Nothing is sent off your device for them. You can switch them off in Settings → Explanations; the app then shows a standard explanation it generates itself.

<!-- fact:P-AI-3 -->
Private Cloud Compute is off by default. It is used only if you turn it on in Settings → Explanations → Private Cloud Compute and confirm the information screen shown before it is enabled.

<!-- fact:P-AI-7 -->
Even when it is on, Private Cloud Compute is used only for longer Premium texts. Short explanations stay on your device.

<!-- fact:P-AI-4 -->
When Private Cloud Compute is used, the app sends a compact summary of the already-calculated chart: the election's local date and time with its UTC offset, the intent category and type of operation, positions and houses of the significator and the Moon, dignity scores, planetary day and hour, risk status and flagged risks, Moon condition, operator rating, the main reasons for the result and the classification your plan may show. The identifier of the entity you chose and its compatibility level are included only when the requested text explains that compatibility.

<!-- fact:P-AI-5 -->
The following are never sent to Private Cloud Compute: coordinates, place name, time-zone name, the ritual description you typed, notes, and any account or device identifier.

<!-- fact:P-AI-6 -->
Apple processes Private Cloud Compute requests on Apple servers designed for privacy; Apple states that the data is used only to fulfil the request and is not stored or made accessible to Apple. See https://security.apple.com/blog/private-cloud-compute/ and Apple's privacy policy.

<!-- fact:P-AI-8 -->
Your Private Cloud Compute consent is recorded on your device with the version of the information screen you confirmed and the time. Turning Private Cloud Compute off deletes that record and stops its use immediately. If we change the information materially, the app asks again.

<!-- fact:P-AI-9 -->
No feature requires Private Cloud Compute. If it is off or unavailable (for example without a network connection, after Apple's daily limit, or on unsupported devices or regions), explanations come from Apple Intelligence on your device or from the app's standard explanation.

<!-- fact:P-AI-10 -->
The app does not use any other AI provider and contains no AI service keys.

## 6. Reports you export

<!-- fact:P-EX-1 -->
Reports (PDF, text, or an Election Magick data file) are created on your device only when you ask for them. They leave your device only if you share or save them yourself using the iOS share sheet; from then on, the service you choose (for example e-mail or a cloud drive) handles them under its own terms.

<!-- fact:P-EX-2 -->
PDF and text reports contain the calculated election, the place name, its time zone and the ritual description you typed, but not the place's coordinates.

<!-- fact:P-EX-3 -->
The Election Magick data file (.emreport) additionally contains the inputs needed to reproduce the calculation, including the place's coordinates. Share it only with people you want to have that information.

<!-- fact:P-EX-4 -->
Temporary copies created for sharing are protected by iOS file protection and deleted after one hour.

## 7. Network connections, diagnostics and advertising

<!-- fact:P-NET-1 -->
The app itself connects only to Apple services: the App Store (subscriptions), Apple Maps (city search and place names) and, if you enable it, Private Cloud Compute. Links you tap (support e-mail, our website, Apple's licence agreement, subscription management, refund requests) open outside the app.

<!-- fact:P-DIAG-1 -->
The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software. If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us as Apple describes; you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

<!-- fact:P-ADS-1 -->
We show no advertising, do not track you across other apps or websites, do not sell or share personal data, and there is no account to create.

## 8. Keeping and deleting your information

<!-- fact:P-RET-1 -->
Your information stays on your device until you delete it. We keep no copy. Temporary export copies are deleted after one hour.

<!-- fact:P-DEL-1 -->
Settings → Delete local data removes saved elections, places, favourites, ritual profiles, history, the chosen location, the record of your legal acceptance, the onboarding state and your Private Cloud Compute consent. The consultation digests described in section 4 remain. Deleting the app removes its data from your device, except items iOS keeps in the Keychain.

More detail: Data Retention Policy (in the app under Settings → Legal).

## 9. Children

<!-- fact:P-KIDS-1 -->
The app is not directed at children and is not designed for use by children.

Because we do not hold your personal data, you exercise these rights directly in the app (view, export or delete your data as described above). For any question or complaint, contact [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. You may also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (UK), the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (Brazil) or the authority of your EU/EEA country.

If we change this policy materially, the app shows the new version and asks you to acknowledge it before continuing. The version and effective date are shown at the top.
