## How it works
- Describe your intention and choose the place: every calculation uses that place's coordinates and time zone, local sunrise and sunset, houses and planetary hours.
- Find an Election searches a date range; Analyse a Moment checks a specific date and time.
- Each result is screened for risks first (combustion, retrogradation, debility, Algol, the South Node, hard aspects of Mars and Saturn, a compromised Ascendant ruler, the Moon's condition) and only then classified and ranked.
- At least five complete elections are always compared before the best one is shown.

## Transparent results
- Chart wheel with tappable planets, essential and accidental dignity, the Moon's applying aspects to the sign change, the operator (Ascendant ruler) and planetary day and hour.
- A full risk audit with the rule behind every finding.
- Methodology and sources inside the app. Nothing is hidden behind a score.
- Reports as PDF, text or a data file you keep.

## Global and private
- Works anywhere: choose a city or use your location (optional). Times are shown in the place's own time zone; at polar latitudes the app tells you when planetary hours are undefined instead of inventing them.
- All calculations run on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
- Optional explanations by Apple Intelligence on your device; they explain results and never change them.

## Entity library
Spirits and deities from historical grimoires and traditions, with every statement marked by its source level.

## Subscriptions
Calculations require a subscription. Standard and Premium use the same calculation engine; Premium adds the full classification scale, longer searches, more ranked results, the complete audit and more consultations. Billed monthly or annually through your Apple account, renewing automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your account settings.

## A note on the method
Astrology and ritual are presented as a traditional, cultural practice. Results describe how a moment scores within the method; they do not predict or guarantee any outcome.
